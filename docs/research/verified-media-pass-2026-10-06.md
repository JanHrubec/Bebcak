[LEGO Friends: Beach Adventure](https://www.bethanseller.com/lego-friends-beach) belongs to a different production and explicitly credits a different stylist. It was **not** used for Dušan’s Heartlake City project. Armada’s separate LEGO NINJAGO film features three children around a table, unlike the shop/parent-child advert in Dušan’s post, so it was also excluded. Matching only a brand or director is insufficient evidence.

The Instagram Tagged grid did not return posts during this pass, including after navigation through the visible Tagged tab. Known public post URLs continued to open, and their scoped main carousels were reviewed without collecting images from “More posts” recommendations. Pavel Hejný’s public advertising portfolio was checked but did not list this Proud campaign; unrelated Pilsner/other client photographs were not borrowed.

Several original photographs, character-reference collages and reposted films remain limited by the source resolution. Those studies follow the finished campaigns in the curated order and retain natural proportions and native display-size caps. No unverified client, production role, personal award or design-process quotation was added to fill a gap. No incorrectly timed Škoda film was published.
