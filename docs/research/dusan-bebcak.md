# Dušan Bebčák — portfolio research

Initial research: **5 October 2026**, with a photographic follow-up on **6 October 2026 (Europe/Prague)**. Research notes and source evidence for the portfolio implementation. Published summaries are deliberately shorter than these notes.

## Scope and evidence

This dossier records publicly accessible professional information, campaign credits, photographs and statements. **Instagram was successfully read with Playwright on 5 October 2026 after the user logged in. All 47 posts displayed on the profile were opened**, spanning 17 June 2024–4 September 2026, including two collaborative posts authored by other accounts. Captions, visible comments, carousel material and sampled film frames were inspected. This is not an exhaustive archive of Stories, nested comments or complete films. No substantive first-person explanation of costume materials, construction or creative decisions was found in the inspected captions/comments.

The complete source-by-source follow-up is [Instagram post inventory](instagram-posts.md), with [selected factual credits and timestamps](instagram-posts.json), [rendered media references](instagram-media.json) and the expanded [asset source manifest](asset-sources.json). **32 Instagram-served carousel images** were downloaded, alongside browser reference screenshots. The website now uses sourced media; see [publication and media audit](media-audit.md) for the current content, identity corrections and remaining photographic gaps.

Evidence labels used below:

- **Named credit:** an identifiable production, director, artist or industry publication explicitly credits Dušan Bebčák / Dusan Bebcak for the specified work. This verifies what the source says; it is not independent proof that every historical namesake is the Instagram account holder.
- **Handle linked:** the source connects the full name, costume role and `@bebcak` handle. This is stronger identity evidence.
- **Historical professional match:** exact name and relevant styling occupation, but no independently verified connection to the Instagram account.
- **Lead:** source unavailable, only a search excerpt, inconsistent spelling or missing identity evidence. Do not publish as established fact.
- **Visual observation:** my description of an inspected image, not a project title, material specification or statement by Dušan.

Preserve the distinction between costume designer, wardrobe stylist, costume assistant, photographer and production designer. A brand credit belongs to the particular campaign, not all campaigns by that brand.

## Campaign details and attribution checks

### Engelmüller — strongest cross-checked campaign

The campaign promotes a limited glove collection tied to Aero Minor's Le Mans history and a contribution to Le Mans Redux. Médiář dates the campaign and names Dušan for costume design. The producer's and director representation's film credits independently repeat his role. Photographer Pavel Hejný is a useful source for campaign photography, but no separately verified photo gallery was recovered. [Médiář](https://www.mediar.cz/galerie-reklamy/rucne-site-rukavice-engelmuller-pribeh-ceskeho-vozu-na-le-mans/), [Creative Embassy](https://vimeo.com/396010194), [el Colony](https://vimeo.com/434345303).

Médiář also labels Alex Bareš as costume designer, whereas the producer's credit labels him gaffer. Its prose names Scholz & Friends while the current agency taxonomy shows S5IVE. Retain Dušan's corroborated credit; do not silently reproduce the inconsistent fields. The quoted campaign explanation is by Tomáš Kopečný, **not Dušan**. These sources do not establish that Dušan designed or manufactured the advertised gloves.

### AdParáda — credit scope

The [ADC article](https://www.artdirectorsclub.cz/clanek/1-video-adparada-od-creative-embassy) places his credit in the January programme's production block. The subsequent Post Bellum, badminton, Czech Philharmonic and other entries are reviewed campaigns. Do not assign those campaigns to him from this article. Jury opinions are attributed to the named jury members, not Dušan.
