# Raiffeisen and E.ON source review — 11 October 2026

## E.ON

All original photographs and production credits come from [Dušan’s E.ON carousel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQd4pgojHgA/). The file-to-carousel mapping is retained in `published-assets.json`. The caption names VCCP, Mugshots, Michal Babinec, Boogie Films and @anezka_stra. It supports participation through his own portfolio; it does not independently establish design or fabrication responsibility.

The [official E.ON campaign announcement](https://www.eon.cz/pro-media/pro-media-tiskove-zpravy/nechte-to-na-nas,-rika-e-on-v-nove-kampani-s-hercem-martinem-mysickou-od-vccp/) identifies Martin Myšička, Mugshots, Boogie Films and photographer Petr Dobiáš. Three complete official-channel films were downloaded at 1920 × 1080 without re-encoding:

- [Energie vždy za férové ceny](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x18wL2bbb1A): checked blazer and blue denim shirt match the upright photograph.
- [Fotovoltaika s Virtuální baterií](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rUL9N7FOspU): brown leather coat, patterned sweater and denim shirt match image 7.
- [Sdílení elektřiny](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nyEFYpwX1e4): patterned knitwear and blue collar match images 4 and 6; the film also includes the leather-coat outfit.

The knitwear portrait is a finished campaign photograph, not merely a fitting. The former E.ON fitting entry is therefore merged into the campaign, with its old route redirected. Three inspected HD frames replace the two low-resolution portrait film grabs; separately photographed material remains identified separately from film frames.

## Raiffeisen

The shop/workshop photographs come from [Dušan’s carousel DQc4eNJjIJ5](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQc4eNJjIJ5/). Its caption identifies Raiffeisen Bank International, LOCCO, Stink Prague, director Maca Rubio, cinematographer Santi Cantillo and shared wardrobe by Dušan / @u_nik_orn. These credits come from the actual source publication, not a guessed matching advert.

The collection includes workshop and production material; calling every image a fitting was too narrow. It is now named “Raiffeisen — Shop & workshop”. No full film was definitively matched to these garments. The owner ended that search on 11 October; no speculative film is attached, and no claim is made that the work was unreleased or not an advert.

The separate Spooky Vintage Shopping collection retains its matched film and now also records [the original costume-photo carousel DQUSnWNjNPi](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQUSnWNjNPi/). A common client or crew is not sufficient evidence to mix photographs between campaigns.
