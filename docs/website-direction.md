# Portfolio direction

One continuous, image-led collection for Dušan Bebčák. No role filters, year sections or date-based browsing. The archive must not imply inactivity through missing calendar years.

## Current implementation

- 58 attributed collections, 291 gallery images and 50 complete locally playable films. Evidence is either an explicit professional credit or his own @bebcak publication; precise roles are not inferred from an uncaptioned post.
- Three archive columns on desktop, two on tablet and one on phones. Inside projects, natural photo proportions, two-column flow and panoramic spans provide a clean layout. Videos precede the photographs and start muted when visible.
- Permanent translucent desktop sidebar, visible Previous/Next, small neighbouring titles, restrained sliding motion and reduced-motion support. Desktop home contact stays in the sidepanel; phone contact stays on About.
- First-person About, source-backed credits without project descriptions, large photograph controls and no zoom toggle or redundant All work button.
- Exact campaign/film/costume matching, original photographs and clearly evidenced film stills. Three unverified demo-only studies remain in research.
- Pre-rendered content and metadata for each published route, social previews, structured data, favicon/Apple icon, missing-page handling and legacy project redirects.
